What to try (8 patterns) multi-finger + long-press feel untested on a real phone

  1. 1 On the map: 2-finger tap = undo, 3-finger tap = redo. 2-finger drag/pinch = move/zoom.
  2. 2 Sea tab: drag the slider, then slide your finger down off it for 0.25× and 0.05× precision. Or type a number.
  3. 3 Draw tab: draw, then hold still at the end to snap to a line, circle or rectangle.
  4. 4 Right edge: Undo, Redo, Zoom +/−, Move buttons, one finger only.
  5. 5 Place tab: tap to place; long-press a marker (red ring) to delete.
  6. 6 Place tab: turn on Confirm before placing: ghost + ✓ / ✕.
  7. 7 Top-left: layers icon strip; tap an icon to show/hide, ▸ to expand names.
  8. 8 Every control is ≥48 px. The dashed-box button (top right) outlines all target boxes and measures them:
Tap the dashed-box button to measure.
