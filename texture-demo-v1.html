Texture look

The same sample island, five ways. Original procedural art, in the direction of inked pictorial maps and old RPG stamps. Nothing here is traced from Pauline Baynes, Inkarnate, Wonderdraft, or Dungeondraft.

Drawing the five looks…

Ink stamps Height blend RPG stamps Combined Contours

Pictorial ink stamps

Pen hills, mountains, and trees. Relief is lit from the stamp alpha, the way a bump map uses height without moving the surface.

Height-blended fills

Grass, rock, mud, sand, and snow. Each tile keeps a micro-height in its alpha so the raised parts stay clean and the lower texture fills the gaps.

Old-school RPG stamps

Flat realm colors and chunky symbols: triangle peaks, round woods, a keep and a town. A shadow stands in for emboss.

Variant: ink on height-blend

The two recommendations together. Height-blended ground, then the same pen stamps and their relief.

Variant: contour relief

A draft chart. Contour lines follow the height, ink weight changes their spacing, and emboss lifts the strokes.
